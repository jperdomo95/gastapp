## Reports and settings

Reports show where your money went this month and how that compares over time.

![The Reports page with the 6-month trend and spending by category](/guide/reports.png)

| Report | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| This month | Total spent so far this month |
| 6-month trend | Spending per month for the last six months |
| This vs last month | This month's total beside last month's |
| By category | This month's spending per category, with each one's share in percent |
| Top merchants | This month's biggest merchants, by total spent |

**Settings** (the gear icon) hold your timezone, which sets "today" and "this month" across the app. The sun/moon icon beside it switches between light and dark themes. This guide is under the **?** icon next to them.

## Subscriptions

A subscription adds its own charge to your expenses on each billing date, so you never type recurring bills by hand. GastApp adds charges that come due every night, and again whenever you open your expenses.

![The Subscriptions page with suggestions found in your expenses](/guide/subscriptions.png)

**Add one** with **New** on the Subscriptions page: name, amount, frequency (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly or yearly), start date, an optional end date, a category and an optional note. If the start date is in the past, you can tick **Add the charges that already happened since the start date**. Leave it off to start from the next charge.

**Manage them** with the icons on each row:

- **Pause and resume.** A paused subscription adds no charges until you resume it.
- **Edit.** While editing, add a **scheduled price change** with its new amount and the date it takes effect. Charges already added keep their old amount.
- **Delete.** This stops all future charges. Charges it already added stay in your expenses.

The top of the page shows what your subscriptions commit you to per month. You can filter the list by Active, Paused and Archived.

### Recurring-charge finder

On the Active tab, **Found in your expenses** lists charges that repeat on a schedule in expenses you typed or imported: same merchant, similar amount, regular dates. Each suggestion shows how many charges it found since when, and the last or next expected date. A few labels can appear:

- **Looks stopped:** the expected charge is overdue.
- **Possible duplicate:** two live plans from the same merchant at different prices, so you may be paying twice.
- **Irregular:** the dates or amounts vary, so the guess is less certain.

Press **Track** to turn a suggestion into a subscription. It starts from the next expected charge, because the earlier ones are already in your expenses. After that GastApp adds each charge itself, so **skip those lines in future bank imports**, or they count twice. Press **X** if it isn't a subscription. Dismissed suggestions are remembered on that device, and **Show dismissed** brings them back.

## Getting started

GastApp tracks what you spend, what comes in, and how long each paycheck has to last. Four steps get you set up:

1. **Set your timezone** in Settings (the gear icon). It decides what "today" and "this month" mean for the dashboard, reports, the pay cycle and subscription charges. Until you set it, GastApp uses UTC. If your device is in a different zone, Settings offers to switch to it.
2. **Add your salary** under Income, so the dashboard can show how much of this paycheck is left.
3. **Add expenses** one at a time with the **+** button, or import a bank statement CSV on the Expenses page.
4. **Add your subscriptions**, or let the recurring-charge finder suggest them from your expenses.

The main menu has Dashboard, Expenses, Subscriptions, Income, Savings, Categories and Reports. On a phone, the bottom bar holds the main pages and the **+** button for a quick expense.

**Amounts are in US dollars.** GastApp is USD-only for now, and every form saves amounts as USD.

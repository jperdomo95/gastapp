## Set-asides and savings goals

Set-asides are money that leaves each paycheck first. What remains, minus subscriptions, becomes your weekly allowance for day-to-day spending. Savings goals track a target that a savings set-aside pays into.

### Set-asides

Add them under Income, in **Off the top each payday**. Each has a name, an amount per paycheck, and one of three kinds:

![Set-asides under Off the top each payday, with what each has received this cycle](/guide/set-asides.png)

| Kind | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed transfer | Money that goes to someone else every payday, like a partner's share or rent |
| Savings | Money moved to savings first, not with whatever is left |
| Reserve | Money held for surprises, the card bill or irregular costs |

Tell GastApp which expenses pay a set-aside: text in the expense description (at least 3 characters, like the last digits of the account), a category, or both. Matching expenses fill the set-aside instead of counting as spending. Each shows how much of it you've paid this cycle. Whatever isn't paid yet shows on the cycle card as **To set aside**. If you pay in more than planned, the extra comes off your allowance too. When an expense matches two set-asides, it counts toward the first one in the list.

**Weekly allowance.** GastApp takes income, minus set-asides and subscription charges, and spreads it evenly over the cycle's days. It hands that out week by week, starting on payday. The card shows this week's budget and what you've spent from it. Overspending one week comes out of the weeks left.

### Savings goals

Add a goal on the Savings page with **New**: a name, a target, what you've saved so far, optional steps on the way (like 1000; 2500), and the savings set-aside that funds it. Only Savings set-asides can fund a goal.

![The Savings page with a goal, its steps and when each is reached](/guide/savings.png)

- **Progress** grows with every expense that pays the linked set-aside after the date you entered the balance. Transfers dated on or before that day are already in the balance.
- **Dates** for each step use the set-aside's amount as your monthly pace. Without one, they use your recent average once you have 4 weeks of history.
- **Update balance** resets what is saved, as of today, after a withdrawal, interest or a deposit made some other way.
- **Changing or removing the set-aside** keeps everything saved so far. The same goes when you delete the set-aside itself.

A step at or above the target is flagged in the form and isn't kept. To enter several steps, separate them with a space or a semicolon. The dashboard's Top goal card shows your first goal that still has a step to reach.

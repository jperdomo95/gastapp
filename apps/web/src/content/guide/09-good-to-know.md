## Good to know

A few limits and habits that keep your numbers right.

- **USD only.** Every amount is saved as US dollars. Other currencies may come later.
- **One import per statement period.** Imported expenses aren't checked for duplicates, so an overlapping statement adds them twice. Deposits are checked.
- **Tracked subscriptions add their own charges.** After you track one, skip its lines in later bank imports.
- **The deposit list shows your 200 most recent deposits.** The page says so when it reaches that limit.
- **The recurring-charge finder** looks back about two years. It needs at least 3 charges for weekly to monthly plans, and 2 for quarterly or yearly ones.
- **Set your timezone** early. Until you do, "today" is UTC, which can put subscription charges and the pay cycle's "today" a day off from yours.

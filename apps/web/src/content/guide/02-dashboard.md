## Dashboard

The dashboard answers two questions: how much have I spent this month, and will this paycheck last until the next one?

![The dashboard with this month's spending, the pay-cycle card and the top goal](/guide/dashboard.png)

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Spent this month | Total spent so far this month, and how it compares with last month in percent. |
| This paycheck | What is left of the current paycheck, how much you can spend per day, this week's allowance, the forecast and the burn chart (see [Income and pay cycle](#income-and-pay-cycle)). Until you add a salary it shows a **Set up** link instead. |
| Top goal | Your first savings goal that still has a step to reach. Hidden when you have no goals. |
| Avg / day and Total entries | Average daily spending this month, and how many expenses you have recorded. |
| By category | A donut chart of this month's spending by category. |
| Monthly trend | Spending per month over recent months. |
| Upcoming this month | Active subscriptions still due to charge this month, with their total. |
| Recent | Your five most recent expenses. |
